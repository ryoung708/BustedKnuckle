Built from shop-floor experience

Built by a technician. For technicians.

IronKnuckle is being built around a simple shop rule: don't guess, don't throw parts, and don't call it fixed until the evidence supports the repair. The goal is to turn hard-earned diagnostic experience into a tool technicians can learn from and contribute to.

Development mode
Technical content quality gate
Not customer-ready

Technical content has to earn its place here. Fault guidance must be checked against the correct engine, emissions family, model year and authorized source before it becomes repair advice. A code alone is not proof of a failed part.
