Follow the torque

With the clutch engaged, engine torque enters the input shaft. The input gear drives both countershafts. The selected front-section gear transfers torque to the mainshaft, then through the auxiliary range section to the output.

  • Watch both countershafts turn in opposite directions.
  • Select one of five main-box positions.
  • Change low/high range to double the five-position pattern.

What moves during a manual shift

  • The driver operates the clutch pedal to disconnect engine torque while shifting.
  • The shift lever moves a mechanical selector rail and fork.
  • A sliding clutch collar engages the selected gear; this conventional 10-speed teaching layout is unsynchronized.
  • The driver preselects low or high range. The air-shifted auxiliary range change occurs as the shift lever passes through neutral.
