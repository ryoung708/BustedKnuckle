Follow the torque

With the clutch engaged, engine torque enters the transmission through the input shaft. The input drive gear turns the countershaft. A selected gear pair transfers torque to the mainshaft, which drives the output.

  • Watch the input and countershaft rotate.
  • Select a gear to move its engagement sleeve.
  • Release the clutch to see torque transfer stop.
Training concept only. Real AMT layouts, shift strategies, air circuits, sensors and gear counts vary. Use vehicle-specific authorized service information for specifications and repair decisions.

What moves when a shift is commanded

  • The transmission controller requests a gear and coordinates engine torque reduction with clutch release.
  • A pneumatic clutch actuator operates the clutch release mechanism; the model shows the cylinder, rod and release fork.
  • Shift-control air cylinders move selector rails. Each rail carries a fork that slides a synchronizer sleeve.
  • The sleeve locks the selected free-running gear to the mainshaft; the clutch then re-engages and torque flows to the output.

Teaching model boundaries

This is an original simplified AMT teaching architecture. It is not a dimensional model of a specific transmission. Shaft count, gear count, selector arrangement, clutch actuation, air circuits and control strategy vary by design. Use the applicable OEM service information for exact component placement and calibration.
