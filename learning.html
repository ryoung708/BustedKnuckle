The bay-side reference

Understand the system before testing it.

Use the diagram to understand the path, the component to understand its job, and the correct test to confirm what is happening. A symptom is a starting point—not a parts diagnosis.

⚡

Electrical

Trace the complete path: source → protection → control → load → ground.

  • Verify power and ground under the correct conditions.
  • Use voltage drop to find resistance under load.
  • Isolate power before resistance or continuity tests.
⚙

Engine Basics

Follow air, fuel, compression and exhaust through the four-stroke cycle.

  • Intake, compression, power and exhaust.
  • Mechanical condition and control inputs both matter.
  • Compare actual values with the correct OEM procedure.
≋

Aftertreatment

Follow exhaust from the engine through DOC, DPF and SCR.

  • Understand temperature, pressure and NOx feedback.
  • Check enable conditions before forcing a regeneration.
  • Separate sensor, dosing, restriction and catalyst concerns.
